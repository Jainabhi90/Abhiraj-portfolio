## Deliberate differences and remaining limits

- No personal portraits. The NioLearn image contains its product's illustrated mascot, not a personal photograph.
- Five projects rather than copying the reference owner's content. Two projects without verified screenshots retain explicitly labeled editorial graphics.
- Direct email rather than a contact form with no delivery service.
- Darker green on paper where required for readable text contrast.
- Retained the existing framework and animation architecture. The stylesheet still contains older overridden rules and merits consolidation in a later maintenance pass.
- No claim that every pixel, every timing curve, or every external browser/device combination is identical to the references. Those assertions would exceed the evidence.
